# Data profile

Generated by `scripts/profile_data.py` from the S-Pankki CSVs in `data/`. Re-run it to refresh. Field meanings are in [CHALLENGE.md](CHALLENGE.md#summary-brief-s-pankki-pdf); how we load the data is in [SETUP.md](SETUP.md).

Amounts: negative = money out, positive = money in. "Spent" columns show money out as a positive number.

## Key findings for the coach

- **Every category has exactly one transaction type** and the 36 merchant codes split the 8 card-purchase categories into finer merchant types (e.g. Transportation → fuel, parking, public transport, taxis). That is the finest level we can classify at: there are no merchant names, locations or free-text descriptions.
- **Amounts don't differ by merchant type within a category**: e.g. every Transportation merchant type has a median of about 41 €, so a taxi costs the same as a tank of fuel. The data is synthetic; merchant types are useful as labels and for counting visits, not for telling expensive from cheap habits.
- **Large one-off purchases exist in every category** (0.1% of purchases are 20× their category's median). They are the natural content for the 🔧 unexpected basket and for "what happened this month?" explanations.
- **Regular rhythm:** salary arrives once a month, usually from the 15th; housing is paid once a month in the first week; utilities about 3 times a month. Safe-to-spend can count what is still due before the next salary.
- **Money is tight for many customers:** median income left after spending is 8.8%, a quarter of customers keep 1.4% or less, and 69.1% have been overdrawn at some point. Safe-to-spend matters.
- **Salary sweeps affect only 5.2% of customers**, but for them most of the salary leaves the account, so their regular costs may be paid elsewhere.
- **Customers are mostly 35–54** and all live in the Helsinki region (postal areas 00, 01, 02). Young adults (18–24), whom S-Bank highlights, are 8.2%.
- **Mortgage data covers only 200 customers** (April–May 2026): usable for a "could I afford a home?" feature, not for the core coach.
