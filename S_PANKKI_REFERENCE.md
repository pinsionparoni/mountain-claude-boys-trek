# S-Pankki AI financial coach: vocabulary, voice and topic rules

Research reference for a Finnish- and English-language hackathon prototype inside S-mobiili. Public-source checks were performed on 14 November 2026; this document distinguishes observed bank wording, proposed prototype wording and legal requirements.

The recommended prototype scope is descriptive spending analysis, customer-controlled budgeting and general financial education, with a clear handoff to approved bank processes for personal investment recommendations and credit decisions. This is a design recommendation, not a statement of S-Pankki policy or legal approval; personal investment recommendations and automated investment services remain subject to the relevant investment-service obligations. ([Finanssivalvonta: investment services](https://www.finanssivalvonta.fi/finanssisektorin-toimijalle/paaomamarkkinat/sijoituspalvelun-tarjoajat/sijoituspalvelut-tai-sijoitustoiminta/), [EU delegated regulation, Articles 9 and 54](http://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/AUTO/?uri=celex:32017R0565))

## Important findings before you build

- **English is not purely hypothetical:** S-Pankki’s English onboarding page says S-mobiili supports an English language selection from version 2.31.0, under “Minä (Me)”, while online banking remains available in Finnish and Swedish and the bank primarily serves customers in those languages. Do not assume that every underlying service, document or human-support journey is available in English. ([S-Pankki: Welcome to S-Bank](https://www.s-pankki.fi/fi/tule-asiakkaaksi/welcome-to-s-bank))
- **S-Pankki already has a virtual assistant:** its October 2026 description presents the S-mobiili assistant as a 24/7 navigation and service-routing chatbot, with other digital channels or customer service as fallbacks; it describes more personalised AI-agent functionality as a development direction, not proof that today’s assistant analyses spending. ([S-Pankki: S-mobiili assistant, 15 October 2026](https://www.s-pankki.fi/fi/tiedotteet/2026/lahes-joka-kolmas-suomalainen-hoitaa-viikoittain-pankkiasioitaan-s-mobiilissa--kayttajien-maara-tuplaantunut-viidessa-vuodessa/))
- **A disclaimer is not a regulatory shield:** an apparently personalised recommendation to buy, sell or hold an identified fund can be investment advice even if the message says it is not advice. ([Finanssivalvonta: investment advice boundary](https://www.finanssivalvonta.fi/finanssisektorin-toimijalle/paaomamarkkinat/sijoituspalvelun-tarjoajat/sijoituspalvelut-tai-sijoitustoiminta/))
- **Two referral corrections matter:** Finnish public financial and debt counselling is currently provided through the state Legal Services Authority, not a municipal service to be labelled generically as such; MIELI’s English crisis helpline ended on 23 March 2026 and was replaced by other English-language support formats. ([Legal Services Authority: general advice](https://www.oikeuspalveluvirasto.fi/talous-ja-velkaneuvonta/palvelumme/yleinen-neuvonta/), [MIELI: English support changes](https://mieli.fi/en/news/new-ways-to-receive-support-in-english/))
- **An imminent credit-law change needs review:** amendments to Chapters 7 and 7a of Finland’s Consumer Protection Act take effect on 20 November 2026, six days after this research date, with transitional provisions for existing agreements. Treat launch date and agreement date as compliance inputs. ([Finlex: Act 31/2026](https://www.finlex.fi/en/legislation/collection/2026/31))

## S-Pankki tone of voice

### Observed voice and recommended adaptation

- **Formality and “you”:** the sampled Finnish consumer pages consistently address the reader directly with sinuttelu, including possessive endings and second-person verbs, rather than formal Te; use neutral standard Finnish “sinä”, not an invented bank-wide “sä” persona. The English page similarly uses direct “you” and “we”; English does not have a matching sinuttelu/teitittely distinction. ([S-Pankki: S-mobiili](https://www.s-pankki.fi/fi/verkkopalvelut/s-mobiili/), [English onboarding](https://www.s-pankki.fi/fi/tule-asiakkaaksi/welcome-to-s-bank))
- **Brand character:** S-Pankki’s published 2025 positioning is “Suomen mutkattomin pankki”, centred on understanding customer needs and easy, high-quality digital services; its campaign is described as slightly humorous. For a coach, translate that into approachable, useful and uncomplicated, not jokey responses to debt, gambling or distress. ([S-Pankki: brand concept](https://www.s-pankki.fi/fi/tiedotteet/2025/s-pankki-lanseeraa-uuden-brandimainonnan-konseptin--suomen-mutkattomin-pankki-vastaa-taman-paivan-asiakasodotuksiin/), [Kotus: respectful language](https://kotus.fi/virkakieli/))
- **Sentence length and structure:** the sampled product pages use short headings, action verbs, lists and direct instructions, although explanations and legal conditions are longer. No public numerical sentence-length rule was verified; as an editorial prototype rule, aim for one main idea per sentence, a brief answer first and details on request, without deleting important conditions. ([S-Pankki: S-mobiili](https://www.s-pankki.fi/fi/verkkopalvelut/s-mobiili/), [payments](https://www.s-pankki.fi/fi/tilit/maksaminen/), [Kotus: clear and understandable language](https://kotus.fi/virkakieli/))
- **Advice:** common constructions include “voit”, “kannattaa”, “valitse” and concrete next actions, often connected to the customer’s circumstances. Prefer an option and its consequences over a command that claims to know the customer’s priorities; a personalised recommendation about a particular investment still requires the regulated advice workflow regardless of its friendly phrasing. ([S-Pankki: unexpected expenses](https://www.s-pankki.fi/fi/artikkelit/nain-varaudut-yllattaviin-menoihin/), [payments](https://www.s-pankki.fi/fi/tilit/maksaminen/), [Finanssivalvonta: advice boundary](https://www.finanssivalvonta.fi/finanssisektorin-toimijalle/paaomamarkkinat/sijoituspalvelun-tarjoajat/sijoituspalvelut-tai-sijoitustoiminta/))
- **Warnings:** card guidance uses direct safety instructions, while loan and investment pages explain costs, uncertainty and potential losses. State the consequence and the safe next step plainly; do not bury them in a footer or dilute a serious warning with encouragement. ([S-Pankki: cards](https://www.s-pankki.fi/fi/kortit/), [S-Laina](https://www.s-pankki.fi/fi/lainat/s-laina/), [investment risks](https://www.s-pankki.fi/fi/saastaminen-ja-sijoittaminen))
- **Encouragement:** current guidance stresses starting with small sums, planning ahead and contacting the bank early when repayments are worrying. Some older editorial wording is more admonishing, so the recommended coach adaptation is specifically non-judgmental: describe the pattern, acknowledge uncertainty and offer a manageable next step rather than calling a purchase wasteful. ([S-Pankki: unexpected expenses](https://www.s-pankki.fi/fi/artikkelit/nain-varaudut-yllattaviin-menoihin/), [payment difficulties](https://www.s-pankki.fi/fi/lainat/maksuvaikeudet/), [older budgeting article](https://www.s-pankki.fi/fi/artikkelit/raha-asiat-jarjestykseen/))
- **Typical service wording:** preserve the verified brand and navigation terms “S-mobiili”, “S-Tili”, “S-Etukortti Visa”, “Säästäjä”, “Pankki”, “Sijoitukset” and “Minä” where the relevant source uses them. Verify the actual navigation and English locale strings with the mentors rather than inventing a translated deep link or presenting a website instruction as a guaranteed app path. ([S-Pankki: S-mobiili](https://www.s-pankki.fi/fi/verkkopalvelut/s-mobiili/), [fund-saving navigation](https://www.s-pankki.fi/fi/saastaminen-ja-sijoittaminen), [English language setting](https://www.s-pankki.fi/fi/tule-asiakkaaksi/welcome-to-s-bank))
- **English voice:** follow the English page’s direct, practical customer address but use naturally edited English rather than reproducing occasional awkward wording. Keep product brands unchanged and explain Finnish concepts on first use, for example “Bonus, your purchase-based S Group reward”; a small English sample is not evidence of a comprehensive bilingual house style. ([S-Pankki: English onboarding](https://www.s-pankki.fi/fi/tule-asiakkaaksi/welcome-to-s-bank), [S Group: benefits](https://www.s-kanava.fi/bonus/maksutapaetu/))
- **Public style-guide finding:** targeted searches did not identify a public S-Pankki or S Group editorial/tone-of-voice manual that could be treated as authoritative writing instructions. S-Pankki publishes brand positioning and S Group provides a public image/logo bank, but neither inspected page provides a writing guide; an internal guide may exist and should be requested. ([S-Pankki: brand concept](https://www.s-pankki.fi/fi/tiedotteet/2025/s-pankki-lanseeraa-uuden-brandimainonnan-konseptin--suomen-mutkattomin-pankki-vastaa-taman-paivan-asiakasodotuksiin/), [S Group: image bank](https://s-ryhma.fi/mediapalvelu/kuvapankki))

### Finnish clarity guidance

- **Kotus “hyvä virkakieli”:** the central principles are appropriate, clear and understandable language: respect the reader, use graspable words and sentences, start from familiar concepts and make the reasoning visible. This guidance concerns authorities; it is a useful editorial benchmark for a bank, not proof that S-Pankki is an authority subject to the Administrative Procedure Act’s language provision in its ordinary customer communications. ([Kotus: official language](https://kotus.fi/virkakieli/))
- **Selkeä yleiskieli versus selkokieli:** clear standard Finnish is not automatically selkokieli, which is deliberately adapted in vocabulary, content and structure for readers who need easier language. Label the prototype “selkeä suomi” unless a competent review supports a selkokieli claim; an English translation alone does not solve Finnish reading-comprehension needs. ([Kotus: clear language and easy language](https://kotus.fi/virkakieli/hyvan-virkakielen-hyodyt/selkeaa-kielta-ja-selkokielta/))
- **Finanssiala ry:** its responsible-finance principles call for clear, truthful marketing, advice and communication, clear explanations of service terms and intended needs, and attention to the customer’s financial situation. These are industry principles, not a separate statute or an S-Pankki-specific copy manual. ([Finance Finland: responsible financial-sector principles](https://www.finanssiala.fi/aiheet/vastuullisen-finanssialan-teesit/))
- **Practical writing rule:** explain the difficult term where it appears, give a date range for every spending comparison and state what the calculation includes before drawing a conclusion. This is a recommended implementation of Kotus’s reader-centred reasoning and the industry’s clarity principle, not a quoted bank requirement. ([Kotus](https://kotus.fi/virkakieli/), [Finance Finland](https://www.finanssiala.fi/aiheet/vastuullisen-finanssialan-teesit/))

### Investment information and regulated advice

- **The legal test:** MiFID II’s investment-advice definition, elaborated in Article 9 of Delegated Regulation 2017/565, concerns a personal recommendation about a transaction in a particular financial instrument, presented as suitable for the person or based on their circumstances; the covered actions include buying, selling, subscribing, redeeming and **holding**, not just making a new purchase. A recommendation issued exclusively to the public is not a personal recommendation under that provision. ([EU delegated regulation, Article 9](http://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/AUTO/?uri=celex:32017R0565))
- **The Finnish practical interpretation:** Finanssivalvonta says the advice can be personal if the customer gets the impression that their circumstances were considered, and identifies financial situation, risk tolerance and investment objectives as relevant circumstances. Its examples distinguish general guidance to save monthly in funds or diversify geographically from advice on named instruments; a non-personal publication can still be a separately regulated investment recommendation. ([Finanssivalvonta: investment services and recommendations](https://www.finanssivalvonta.fi/finanssisektorin-toimijalle/paaomamarkkinat/sijoituspalvelun-tarjoajat/sijoituspalvelut-tai-sijoitustoiminta/))
- **Recommended non-advisory boundary:** allow “A fund pools investors’ money” and “Diversification reduces concentration risk”; block “Based on your salary and spending, put €200 a month into [named fund]” and “Keep holding [named fund]; it is right for you” unless the bank has explicitly integrated this into its approved advice process. Rewording the recommendation as a question, a nudge or a disclaimer does not remove the underlying personal-recommendation risk. ([Finanssivalvonta: substance and impression determine advice](https://www.finanssivalvonta.fi/finanssisektorin-toimijalle/paaomamarkkinat/sijoituspalvelun-tarjoajat/sijoituspalvelut-tai-sijoitustoiminta/), [EU Article 9](http://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/AUTO/?uri=celex:32017R0565))
- **The licensing nuance:** the relevant issue is the bank’s authorised service and compliance framework, not whether the model itself has a personal “financial-advisor licence”. A bank may provide investment services under the relevant rules, and automated or semi-automated delivery does not reduce the firm’s responsibility for suitability; required information includes knowledge/experience, objectives and risk tolerance, and financial ability to bear the relevant risk. ([Finanssivalvonta: authorisation and credit institutions](https://www.finanssivalvonta.fi/finanssisektorin-toimijalle/paaomamarkkinat/sijoituspalvelun-tarjoajat/sijoituspalvelut-tai-sijoitustoiminta/), [EU delegated regulation, Article 54](http://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/AUTO/?uri=celex:32017R0565))
- **No unapproved shortcut through Säästäjä:** S-Pankki’s own site describes Säästäjä as providing an investment recommendation, so a non-advisory coach should hand off into that approved journey rather than imitate its recommendation logic with inferred spending data. Do not promise a specific fund is safe, suitable or guaranteed to outperform. ([S-Pankki: saving services and risk disclosures](https://www.s-pankki.fi/fi/saastaminen-ja-sijoittaminen), [Finanssivalvonta: advice boundary](https://www.finanssivalvonta.fi/finanssisektorin-toimijalle/paaomamarkkinat/sijoituspalvelun-tarjoajat/sijoituspalvelut-tai-sijoitustoiminta/))

### Consumer loans and responsible lending

- **Responsible lending applies to conversational marketing too:** KKV’s guidance prohibits minimising borrowing, presenting credit as a solution to financial problems or stress, linking credit to gambling, targeting people likely to have repayment difficulties and reminding customers of unused credit outside the specified exception. Therefore, a spending coach should not respond to a budget shortfall with “You still have €1,500 credit available” or “Take a loan so you can stop worrying”; use budgeting, bank hardship support and independent debt advice instead. ([KKV: offering consumer credit](https://www.kkv.fi/kuluttaja-asiat/tietoa-ja-ohjeita-yrityksille/kuluttaja-asiamiehen-linjaukset/kuluttajaluottojen-tarjoaminen/))
- **Affordability is not the same as a spending forecast:** before granting credit, the lender must assess creditworthiness using sufficient, verified information about income and financial circumstances, including relevant debt and credit-register information. A model that sees one bank account cannot safely announce “You qualify”, “You can afford this loan” or a guaranteed borrowing amount; describe a budget scenario as an estimate and send applications to the bank’s credit process. ([KKV: creditworthiness and verification](https://www.kkv.fi/kuluttaja-asiat/tietoa-ja-ohjeita-yrityksille/kuluttaja-asiamiehen-linjaukset/kuluttaja-asiamiehen-linjaukset/kuluttajaluottojen-tarjoaminen/), [S-Pankki: indicative loan calculations](https://www.s-pankki.fi/fi/lainat/s-laina/))
- **Costs and comparisons:** when credit marketing gives an interest rate, another cost figure or agreement terms, KKV requires the relevant representative-example information, including APR, costs, amount/limit, duration, total payable and instalments, presented clearly as a concise whole. Do not claim refinancing saves money merely because it lowers the monthly instalment, and do not assume that putting the missing disclosures behind a link fixes the advertisement. ([KKV: advertising disclosures and refinancing claims](https://www.kkv.fi/kuluttaja-asiat/tietoa-ja-ohjeita-yrityksille/kuluttaja-asiamiehen-linjaukset/kuluttajaluottojen-tarjoaminen/))
- **Payment difficulties:** explain existing contract options accurately and route the customer to the bank before claiming a due-date change or repayment holiday is approved. S-Pankki notes that principal-repayment holidays can leave interest and charges payable and that some changes require case-by-case agreement; an empathetic message must not promise a contractual concession. ([S-Pankki: payment difficulties](https://www.s-pankki.fi/fi/lainat/maksuvaikeudet/))
- **Rules from 20 November 2026:** Act 31/2026 adds Chapter 7, section 8a’s requirement for a clear, prominent credit-cost warning in consumer-credit advertising, with its detailed content specified by government decree; it also strengthens creditworthiness provisions and rights when assessment involves automated personal-data processing. The latter include a clear explanation, presenting one’s view and requesting reconsideration; final production warning text and transition treatment should be approved by S-Pankki counsel rather than generated by the coach. ([Finlex: Act 31/2026, sections 8a and 14 and commencement provisions](https://www.finlex.fi/en/legislation/collection/2026/31))

### Taxes

- **Explain, but do not issue a tax ruling:** the sources reviewed do not establish a MiFID-style blanket prohibition on general tax explanations by a bank chatbot; the practical boundary is to avoid claiming an uncertain, personalised tax outcome is definitive or binding. For a consequential personal case, link to the Tax Administration or qualified tax support, and explain that a Tax Administration advance ruling is a paid, binding decision for the specified individual tax matter, subject to its facts and conditions, not something a bank coach can provide. ([Finnish Tax Administration: advance rulings](https://vero.fi/yritykset-ja-yhteisot/yhteistyo-ja-palvelut/ennakkoratkaisu-tai-poikkeuslupa/))
- **Recommended tax guardrail:** do not tell a customer “This purchase is definitely deductible”, “You owe no tax” or “Sell this fund now to reduce your taxes” solely from transaction labels. Separate a sourced general tax explanation from a customer-specific calculation, identify the tax year and missing facts, and recognise that a personalised recommendation to sell an identified fund can also cross the investment-advice boundary. ([Finnish Tax Administration: individual facts and rulings](https://vero.fi/yritykset-ja-yhteisot/yhteistyo-ja-palvelut/ennakkoratkaisu-tai-poikkeuslupa/), [EU Article 9: sale recommendations](http://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/AUTO/?uri=celex:32017R0565))

### AI, privacy and sensitive topics

- **AI transparency and accountability:** ESMA’s statement specifically covers AI in retail investment services, including chatbot interactions, and calls for transparent AI-use disclosure, clear/fair/non-misleading information, governance, testing, monitoring and protection of client information. Management remains responsible; using a third-party model does not shift the regulated firm’s responsibility away. ([ESMA: AI and investment services, particularly paragraphs 2 and 8–22](https://www.esma.europa.eu/sites/default/files/2024-05/ESMA35-335435667-5924__Public_Statement_on_AI_and_investment_services.pdf))
- **Current AI Act timetable:** the Commission’s current overview says transparency rules apply from August 2026 and people using chatbots should know that they are interacting with a machine; Finanssivalvonta’s current page specifies 2 August 2026 and a grace period to 2 December 2026. The current official pages put the Annex III high-risk timetable at 2 December 2027, with the Commission describing AI Omnibus changes; do not rely on older summaries that simply say all high-risk obligations began in August 2026. ([European Commission: AI Act and current timetable](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/regulatory-framework-ai), [Finanssivalvonta: AI supervision](https://www.finanssivalvonta.fi/tekoalyn-kayton-valvonta/))
- **Budgeting is not automatically credit scoring:** Finanssivalvonta identifies systems assessing creditworthiness or determining credit scores as relevant high-risk financial uses; a descriptive spending assistant should not silently evolve into such a decision engine. Require a fresh legal/risk assessment before its outputs influence credit approval, limits or eligibility, rather than asserting that every banking chatbot is automatically high-risk or automatically exempt. ([Finanssivalvonta: high-risk AI uses](https://www.finanssivalvonta.fi/tekoalyn-kayton-valvonta/), [European Commission: risk-based approach](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/regulatory-framework-ai))
- **Lawful and limited use of transactions:** identify a lawful basis and purpose, minimise inputs and retention, maintain accuracy and protect confidentiality; consent is not the only possible lawful basis, nor is existing access to a bank account unlimited permission for new AI profiling. Assess whether the proposed processing requires a data-protection impact assessment before introducing high-risk profiling or large-scale special-category processing. ([GDPR, Articles 5, 6 and 35](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/uk/ALL/?uri=CELEX:32016R0679))
- **Sensitive inferences:** transaction-derived health, religious, political or other special-category information can trigger Article 9 issues, and significant solely automated decisions engage Article 22 and its exceptions/safeguards. Recommended coach policy is therefore to report an observable spending pattern without diagnosing addiction, mental illness or another sensitive trait, and never use a gambling or crisis disclosure as a lending-sales signal. ([GDPR, Articles 9 and 22](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/uk/ALL/?uri=CELEX:32016R0679), [KKV: vulnerable-customer and gambling marketing restrictions](https://www.kkv.fi/kuluttaja-asiat/tietoa-ja-ohjeita-yrityksille/kuluttaja-asiamiehen-linjaukset/kuluttajaluottojen-tarjoaminen/))
- **Recommended crisis behaviour:** pause ordinary coaching, respond briefly and without judgment, route immediate danger to 112 and offer the appropriate current human support service; do not claim to diagnose, treat, guarantee safety or have contacted help unless a verified process actually did so. This is a proposed safety policy based on the services’ stated roles, not a publicly verified policy of S-Pankki or its competitors. ([MIELI: crisis support and emergencies](https://mieli.fi/tukea-ja-apua/kriisipuhelin/), [Peluuri: confidential gambling support](https://www.peluuri.fi/peluuri/peluurin-auttava-puhelin-ja-chat))
- **Recommended account-action boundary:** distinguish answering from acting, require the bank’s approved authentication and confirmation flow before changing payment instructions or settings, and never ask for a PIN in free text. S-Pankki distinguishes protected, authenticated service messages for personal banking from ordinary guidance and explicitly says to keep card PINs private. ([S-Pankki: digital-service support](https://www.s-pankki.fi/fi/verkkopalvelut/tukea-digipalveluihin/), [card safety](https://www.s-pankki.fi/fi/kortit/))

### Disclosures: typical substance, not a substitute for controls

- **Investment disclosures observed at S-Pankki:** its savings page explains financial risk, possible loss of capital, the fact that past performance does not guarantee future returns, the customer’s responsibility for decisions and tax effects, and that the page’s information is not statutory investment advice. Use a relevant, short disclosure at the point of need, but never use it to justify an otherwise personal product recommendation. ([S-Pankki: investment disclosures](https://www.s-pankki.fi/fi/saastaminen-ja-sijoittaminen), [Finanssivalvonta: disclaimers do not decide classification](https://www.finanssivalvonta.fi/finanssisektorin-toimijalle/paaomamarkkinat/sijoituspalvelun-tarjoajat/sijoituspalvelut-tai-sijoitustoiminta/))
- **Loan disclosures observed at S-Pankki:** its loan page warns that borrowing causes costs and describes calculator results as examples whose final terms depend on the customer’s credit assessment. A prototype calculation should say “estimate, not an offer”; if it becomes advertising or an application journey, use the full legally approved disclosures rather than this short phrase alone. ([S-Pankki: S-Laina](https://www.s-pankki.fi/fi/lainat/s-laina/), [KKV: required credit information](https://www.kkv.fi/kuluttaja-asiat/tietoa-ja-ohjeita-yrityksille/kuluttaja-asiamiehen-linjaukset/kuluttajaluottojen-tarjoaminen/))
- **Suggested onboarding disclosure, original copy:** FI: “Olen tekoälyavustaja. Autan hahmottamaan rahankäyttöäsi ja budjettiasi. En anna henkilökohtaisia sijoitussuosituksia enkä tee luottopäätöksiä.” EN: “I’m an AI assistant. I help you understand your spending and budget. I do not provide personal investment recommendations or make credit decisions.” Use this only if it accurately describes the implemented service; AI transparency and the actual advice/decision functionality matter more than a label. ([European Commission: chatbot transparency](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/regulatory-framework-ai), [Finanssivalvonta: actual investment-advice substance](https://www.finanssivalvonta.fi/finanssisektorin-toimijalle/paaomamarkkinat/sijoituspalvelun-tarjoajat/sijoituspalvelut-tai-sijoitustoiminta/))
- **Suggested tax-estimate disclosure, original copy:** FI: “Tämä on yleistä verotietoa, ei Verohallinnon sitova ratkaisu. Verokohtelu riippuu omasta tilanteestasi ja verovuodesta.” EN: “This is general tax information, not a binding decision from the Finnish Tax Administration. The tax treatment depends on your circumstances and the tax year.” This is a proposed scope statement, not a statutory magic formula or a verified S-Pankki stock disclaimer. ([Finnish Tax Administration: binding individual rulings](https://vero.fi/yritykset-ja-yhteisot/yhteistyo-ja-palvelut/ennakkoratkaisu-tai-poikkeuslupa/))

### What Finnish banks publicly describe about their assistants

These findings concern public descriptions, not live adversarial testing or access to internal system prompts. Absence of a published crisis/off-topic policy does not mean the bank has no policy.

- **S-Pankki, S-mobiili assistant and Aulis:** the October 2026 announcement says the in-app assistant is available 24/7, helps customers find the right place in S-mobiili and redirects to other digital channels or customer service where needed; the public customer-service page also names Aulis as its chat assistant. The inspected descriptions do not publish detailed rules for self-harm, gambling distress, harassment, politics or off-topic refusal, and they do not establish that Aulis and the in-app assistant are exactly the same implementation. ([S-Pankki: in-app assistant](https://www.s-pankki.fi/fi/tiedotteet/2026/lahes-joka-kolmas-suomalainen-hoitaa-viikoittain-pankkiasioitaan-s-mobiilissa--kayttajien-maara-tuplaantunut-viidessa-vuodessa/), [customer service and Aulis](https://www.s-pankki.fi/fi/asiakaspalvelu/))
- **OP, Opotti:** OP’s 2022 Data Balance Sheet describes proactive support for account/card limits, uncovered payments, unsuccessful credit-card payments and overdue insurance premiums, including supported remedial actions. This is dated capability evidence, not confirmation of every 2026 feature; the current chat page yielded no substantive public handling policy, so a current off-topic, crisis, authentication or human-handoff rule cannot be confirmed from those pages. ([OP: Data Balance Sheet 2022](https://www.op.fi/documents/485141/4162016/OP+Financial+Group+Data+balance+sheet+2022+(pdf).pdf/c3d7d0d1-3e6c-48ef-a8d2-15694ef90ddf?t=1678289945789), [OP: current chat entry page](https://www.op.fi/henkiloasiakkaat/asiakaspalvelu/chat))
- **Nordea, Nova:** the current Finnish retail contact page calls the chatbot **Nova**, says it works 24/7 and explains how and where a banking matter can be handled, with handoff to authenticated human chat when needed; it lists human-chat hours as weekdays 08:00–18:00 and Saturdays 10:00–16:00. No detailed off-topic or mental-health/gambling escalation policy was published on the inspected page; the verified pattern is service routing plus authenticated human support, not a demonstrated therapeutic or personal-advice capability. ([Nordea: retail contact and Nova](https://www.nordea.fi/henkiloasiakkaat/tuki/yhteystiedot-henkiloasiakkaat.html))
- **Danske Bank, Danske Chat:** the current Finnish service page describes AI-assisted mobile chat for banking questions around the clock, with expert handoff, and distinguishes personal banking matters in mobile chat from general questions in website chat; it lists weekday expert hours of 09:00–18:00 for daily banking and 09:00–16:00 for loans/investments. The inspected pages do not specify detailed off-topic/crisis refusal rules; the privacy notice discusses AI safeguards, banking secrecy and recording investment-service communications, but that is not proof of a particular chatbot escalation script or of universal recording of every chatbot conversation. ([Danske Bank: customer service](https://danskebank.fi/sinulle/asiakaspalvelu), [privacy notice](https://danskebank.fi/sinulle/asiakaspalvelu/tietosuoja/tietosuojatiedote/henkiloasiakkaat/))
- **Recommended off-topic pattern:** use a brief scope statement and offer a relevant banking task, while allowing safety-critical questions to trigger a support referral rather than a generic refusal. This is an original product recommendation informed by the banks’ published banking-focused scope and human-routing patterns, not an attributed competitor policy. ([S-Pankki: routing](https://www.s-pankki.fi/fi/tiedotteet/2026/lahes-joka-kolmas-suomalainen-hoitaa-viikoittain-pankkiasioitaan-s-mobiilissa--kayttajien-maara-tuplaantunut-viidessa-vuodessa/), [Nordea: human handoff](https://www.nordea.fi/henkiloasiakkaat/tuki/yhteystiedot-henkiloasiakkaat.html), [Danske Bank: chat scope](https://danskebank.fi/sinulle/asiakaspalvelu))

## Open questions for S-Pankki’s mentors

- **Approved scope:** Is the hackathon coach strictly descriptive, or may it enter an approved investment-advice or credit-assessment journey? Who signs off the boundary and the 20 November 2026 credit-law changes?
- **Language and house style:** Can you provide the internal writing guide, approved Finnish/English glossary, actual English S-mobiili locale strings and accessibility/selkokieli requirements?
- **Data and calculations:** Which accounts, cards, loans, Bonus events and pending reservations are available, and what are the approved rules for refunds, own-account transfers, shared accounts, mortgage principal and recurring-income detection?
- **Categories:** What is the actual S-mobiili spending taxonomy, if any, and may customers correct a category or split a supermarket purchase into food, household goods and other items?
- **Privacy and delivery:** Which model environment, logging, retention, data residency, training-use restrictions and sensitive-inference rules are approved, and has the intended processing received a privacy/risk assessment?
- **Escalation:** What are the approved responses and human handoffs for fraud, gambling, debt distress, suicidal intent and complaints, in Finnish and English, including out-of-hours coverage?
- **Actions and maintenance:** Which actions may the coach initiate, what authentication/confirmation is required, who owns the help-service directory and legal disclosures, and what testing is required before a demo becomes a customer pilot?

## Evidence limits

This is a public-source research and prototype-design reference, not legal advice, a bank-approved policy or an audit of live chatbot behaviour. Exact S-mobiili category labels, internal style rules, English support coverage and sensitive-topic scripts remain explicitly unverified where noted.

Some public materials have different ages and scopes: the MobilePay announcement and OP capability example are historical, while the S-Pankki assistant description, current help-service pages, current regulatory overview and forthcoming Finnish credit-law amendments supply the more recent operational context. Where a source was older or a label was proposed, the report does not present it as a verified current app feature.
